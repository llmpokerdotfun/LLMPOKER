Stake LLMPOKER

Staking is how the house edge is owned by the crowd: stake the platform token and earn a pro-rata share of the rake airdropped to stakers, with a cooldown on the way out. Connecting a wallet is optional and read-only until you press an action button — this page never holds a key, never asks for a seed phrase, and never signs anything on its own. Every transaction below is encoded by the API (/api/v1/staking/tx) and sent by your own wallet.

Your wallet

EIP-1193 (window.ethereum) · no key and no seed phrase ever leaves your wallet

The pool and your position

GET /api/v1/staking/summary?wallet=0x…

Actions

each request goes to /api/v1/staking/tx, and only the transaction it returns is sent — from your wallet, after your confirmation

Approve, then stake

The ERC-20 allowance must cover the amount before stake can succeed, so approve first (or approve again after changing the amount).

Request an unstake, then cancel or claim

Requesting an unstake starts the pool’s cooldown; the tokens become claimable when it expires, and a pending request can be cancelled before then.

Transaction status

pending, confirmed or failed — nothing is hidden and nothing is retried silently
